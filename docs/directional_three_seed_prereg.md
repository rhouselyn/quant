# 2018-cutoff three-seed comparison

Freeze `abl2000_base{,_s43,_s44}/best.pt` (epochs 3, 2, 6). Construct shared candidate rankings from the mean of the three available-stock weekly z-scores. Use pre-2018 training, 2018–2020 validation and 2021 onward historical test with a two-anchor maturity gap before each boundary. Before 2018, short eligibility is unknown and assumed for training. From 2018 use the prior-quarter list proxy; an unavailable list yields no short candidates.

Train one MLP and one independent attention head per frozen encoder, using seed-matched initialization and minimum validation BCE over 100 full-batch Adam steps. Input statistics use training only. The MLP learns a joint directional win probability from the old-score Top-50 long and Bottom-50 eligible short candidates. Select 50 jointly across sides by predicted probability and apply global softmax of the probabilities. For the ensemble, average all three probabilities on the common candidates before selecting and weighting.

Attention uses the common old-score Top-40 long and Bottom-10 eligible short stock embeddings and side indicator, with no MLP inputs. One learned token queries the stocks using four-head cross-attention followed by the FFN and one logit. Train on weeks with both complete baskets and mature labels. The binary label is whether the next equal-weight long leg outperformed the next equal-weight short leg after assumed borrow cost. Positive scores allocate one unit of notional to longs; nonpositive scores allocate it to shorts. Within the selected leg, stock weights are equal. The ensemble averages the logits before the zero threshold. Score and report pure long, pure short, and fixed 50/50 books on the same attention candidates as controls. Also report a static book matched to the realized fraction of long weeks as a retrospective exposure diagnostic.

Replay test dates on a common account with the same gross target, 10 bps per traded notional, and 8% annual assumed short fee. An unavailable leg leaves its budget in cash. Missing returns are set to zero and counted in the replay. Selection and trading use the historical proxy dataset and have the same limits as directional_2018_prereg.md. The baseline models share the new heads' validation years and the historical test interval has been previously explored.
