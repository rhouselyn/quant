# 2018-cutoff joint directional and allocation experiment

Freeze `outputs/abl2000_base/best.pt` seed 42, trained before 2018 and selected on 2018–2020 baseline validation. Split new head training at 2018-01-05 and validation at 2021-01-08, leaving two anchor weeks before each boundary for label maturity. Use 2021 onward only for the final historical test. The baseline checkpoint selection and the new heads share validation years; this is a combined validation selection, and the previously explored test is not pristine.

Old baseline score selects up to 50 highest long and 50 lowest short candidates; exclude long candidates from short. Before 2018 the availability of historical eligibility is unknown, so assume eligible as a training proxy. Use previous-quarter available eligibility from 2018 onward, missing quarters imply no confirmed eligible short. Train the shared 129→32→1 direction classifier on all matured training candidates using directional win binary cross entropy, with per-input standardization fit on training alone. Evaluate validation BCE after every step up to 100 Adam steps, select minimum and restore that checkpoint. Use its sigmoid probabilities to select top 50 jointly, and global softmax across probabilities.

Allocation: learned token queries the selected candidate embeddings and direction through four-head cross-attention, residual layer norm and 32→64→32 FFN with residual layer norm. One output logit, trained using binary cross entropy for whether 60/40 earns more than 40/60 on the subsequent return net of an assumed 8% annual short fee. At inference score > 0 selects 60/40; score <= 0 selects 40/60. Keep MLP-derived within-side weights and leave an absent leg's allocation in cash. Optimize validation BCE over 100 Adam steps. Compare the global MLP book, fixed 60/40 and 40/60, and selected allocation using the same candidates, and old-score 40/10 equal as contextual reference. Full drift-aware turnover at 10bps, and 8% annual short holding cost. Missing realized returns are filled zero in evaluation and counted. Historical borrow, execution and retrospective-universe limitations apply.
